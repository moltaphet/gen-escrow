How it works

1

Create & Fund

Buyer specifies the seller, amount, detailed terms, and deadline. Funds are held in the contract immediately.

2

Work & Release

When conditions are met the buyer releases funds. Simple, on-chain, final.

3

Dispute (if needed)

Either party can raise a dispute with written reason + evidence (links, screenshots, etc).

4

AI Consensus Resolution

GenLayer validators independently analyze the terms and evidence using LLMs. They reach consensus on a fair split or winner.

All payouts use a secure pull-payment pattern. After release or resolution, call Claim to withdraw your GEN.
